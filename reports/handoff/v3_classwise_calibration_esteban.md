# V3-A classwise calibration - Esteban

## 1. Executive summary

**Do not promote this calibration. Keep raw V3-A for this comparison and V2 as the
project's frozen fallback.** Calibration improved TRAIN cross-fitted Macro-F1
from **0.459793827 to 0.469540322** (+0.009746495), with positive deltas in all five
outer folds. The single evaluation of the frozen correction on VALID fell from
**0.424111098 to 0.417236296** (-0.006874801). VALID accuracy rose by .007, but
cloud/gym losses outweigh the none/streaming gains under equal class weighting.

Only a bounded three-coefficient post-processing grid was investigated. No new
classifier was trained, no V2/V3-A global blend was added, no evaluator was edited,
and no VALID result selected or revised parameters. No TEST file or labels were
accessed by this experiment. Predictions, probabilities and diagnostic JSON stay
in ignored local outputs; only source, tests and reports are committed.

Branch: `exp/v3-calibration-esteban`. Initial HEAD and merge-base with
`origin/integration/v3-discovery`: `df9fe4135ff812add0e2f6e0733706050b813cc2`.
Remote refs were refreshed without merging. The unrelated untracked
`laracopilot-fluxfuel21/` directory was left alone.

## 2. Hypothesis

Equal weighting of eight F1 scores makes argmax decisions sensitive to systematic
class-specific score imbalance. A small decision correction estimated from TRAIN
OOF might improve Macro-F1 without changing V3-A's 167-feature representation,
its fixed CatBoost recipe, or its existing 75% classifier / 25% heuristic mixture.
Class-frequency matching is a candidate direction, not a theoretically optimal
Macro-F1 rule. True prevalence alone does not determine optimal decision counts.

## 3. Calibration formulation

For the calibration fitting clients only, compute:

```text
r[c] = log((true_count[c] + 5) / (raw_argmax_count[c] + 5))
d = r - mean(r)
d = d / max(1, max(abs(d)) / 0.25)
b = clip(alpha*d + beta*(gym - none) + gamma*(music - streaming), -0.25, 0.25)
w[c] = exp(b[c])
q[c] = p[c]*w[c] / sum_j(p[j]*w[j])
prediction = argmax_c q[c]
```

`gym - none` denotes a vector with +1 at gym, -1 at none, zero elsewhere;
`music - streaming` is defined analogously. Multiplicative weights and additive
log-probability offsets are the same decision rule, so they are not counted as
separate experiments. Exact zero probabilities stay zero; the identity transform
returns an exact copy, including ties and client/class order.

Bounds imply weights in [0.778801, 1.284025]. Common offsets do not affect argmax;
weights are relative decision scores, not calibrated confidence. Per-class
temperatures were not searched: they add slope parameters without evidence that
this is needed. A common temperature alone cannot change argmax.

## 4. Leakage / overfitting safeguards

- Source and TRAIN input SHA-256 hashes match the original V3 OOF provenance.
  Source probability rows exactly match the five original client-stratified
  folds (seed 42); every client has exactly one row and all eight columns.
- The aligned OOF probability table was generated by assembling those existing
  verified artifacts, avoiding unnecessary classifier retraining. V2 and V3-A
  use exactly the same 2,000 TRAIN clients and folds.
- Original V3-A OOF excludes each held-out client's label from its classifier,
  heuristic map and family map. Its training family features use five inner
  client folds. IDs are grouping/alignment keys, never model features.
- Calibration outer evaluation uses 400 clients; its parameter fit uses only
  the other 1,600 TRAIN OOF clients. No outer label enters that fold's calibration
  direction or parameter selection. A label-mutation regression test checks this.
- Within those 1,600 clients, leave-one-original-fold-out calibration CV selects
  coefficients, recomputing the frequency direction on 1,200 clients and predicting
  400. Final selection repeats the analogous CV on all 2,000 OOF clients.
- The frozen protocol and source/input fingerprints are stored before scoring
  VALID. The VALID phase verifies them, persists predictions, then reads VALID
  labels once. An exclusive marker rejects repeated scoring in that output run.
- No TEST phase, extra blend, VALID threshold sweep, or VALID early stopping.
  The original official evaluator is called unchanged.

**Qualification:** this is nested CV of the calibration layer on fixed base OOF
features, not fully nested end-to-end classifier CV. Base models producing other
OOF folds were trained with the calibration outer holdout's clients, creating
indirect dependence. TRAIN results therefore have that limitation even though
calibration never fits its own evaluation rows. V3-A and the targeted contrasts
were also motivated by earlier research using the reused official VALID split;
VALID is not an independent untouched holdout. No claim to an unbiased promotion
estimate or statistically established improvement is made.

## 5. Parameter-selection procedure

The protocol was written before execution in
`reports/handoff/v3_classwise_calibration_protocol_esteban.md`.

1. Evaluate the fixed grid: alpha in {0,.25,.5,.75,1}; beta and gamma each in
   {0,-.075,.075,-.15,.15}, giving 125 candidates including identity.
2. Maximize pooled inner held-out Macro-F1 minus
   `.02 * mean_inner_folds(mean_classes((b/.25)^2))`.
3. Require at least .001 improvement over identity in this regularized objective.
4. Relative to raw inner held-out predictions, reject a candidate losing more
   than .02 F1 in any class, more than .005 in more than two classes, or failing
   to preserve/improve at least four classes. These are selection safeguards;
   they cannot guarantee per-class performance on unseen clients.
5. Break exact ties by fixed enumeration (identity first). Fit the selected
   direction using the whole available calibration fitting partition.

Eight offsets are derived from three searched coefficients and a predefined
frequency estimate. They are not eight independently optimized free parameters.
The final choice was alpha=1, beta=-.15, gamma=-.075. Its inner CV selection
Macro-F1 was 0.477712639; penalty 0.005456157; objective 0.472256482. That is a
selection diagnostic, **not** the reported TRAIN cross-fitted performance.

## 7. VALID

The frozen final correction was evaluated once on the 1,000 VALID clients.
Macro-F1 delta is **-0.006874801**, while accuracy changes **.461 to .468**.
It remains above V2 by .025686840, but raw V3-A is better than the correction.
No alternative coefficients were tried after this result.

None correct predictions rise 176 to 210; all positive-class correct predictions
combined fall 285 to 258. Of 116 changed predictions, 40 become correct, 33 become
incorrect, and 43 remain incorrect with a different wrong label. Of the 40 wins,
34 are true-none clients. This is precisely the accuracy/positive-family tradeoff
that Macro-F1 is intended to expose.

The TRAIN/VALID shift is visible in raw decision frequencies: none is 15.20%
versus 29.85% truth in TRAIN OOF, but already 28.60% versus 29.30% truth on VALID.
Gym is overpredicted on TRAIN (12.65% versus 9.50%) and underpredicted on VALID
(10.50% versus 12.10%). A fixed correction learned from those TRAIN imbalances
therefore moves both classes too far on VALID. This is a descriptive explanation,
not proof of the cause; fold/full-fit mapping differences and sampling variation
can also contribute.

The dominant signs are consistent: none positive, gym negative, streaming
positive, and cloud/insurance/mobile/software negative in all five folds. None
hits the upper .25 bound in every fold; this is boundary pressure, not evidence
that a larger correction should be allowed. The bound was not expanded.

The detailed calibration is **partly unstable**. Music's offset changes sign
(three negative, two positive); gamma switches between -.075 and zero. Gym's
weight ranges roughly .80-.92, and streaming's .5%-9.2% uplift also varies.
No exact coefficient triple is selected in all folds, and the final triple
(1,-.15,-.075) is not selected in any outer fit. This does not show wildly
reversing signs for every class, but it prevents claiming a stable eight-class
correction. Stable TRAIN signs also do not imply transfer to the full-fit VALID
probability distribution.

## 12. Recommendation

**Reject this correction for integration; retain raw V3-A for this arm.** TRAIN
provides modest evidence for a none/gym decision imbalance, but the correction
fails the intended Macro-F1 objective on the already-reused VALID benchmark and
sacrifices gym/cloud. The small accuracy gain is not an adequate substitute.
No VALID-best alternative is substituted for the frozen TRAIN-selected rule.

Keep the lightweight runner and negative result as research evidence. Any future
attempt should first address OOF-to-full-fit score-distribution transfer and
use end-to-end nested base refits or a genuinely new cohort for confirmation;
this report does not authorize another sweep on VALID. No production default,
submission, main branch or integration branch was changed, and no merge was made.
